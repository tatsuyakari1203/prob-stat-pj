\section{Conclusion}

This comprehensive study successfully analyzed the impact of 3D printing parameters on product quality using multiple linear regression analysis. Through systematic examination of 50 observations across 9 input parameters and 3 quality metrics, the research established significant predictive models that provide actionable insights for 3D printing optimization.

\subsection{Key Research Findings}

\paragraph{Model Performance and Predictive Capability}
The analysis yielded three robust predictive models with varying degrees of explanatory power:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Roughness Model}: Achieved exceptional predictive accuracy (R² = 87.17\%, F = 59.78, p < 0.001) with RMSE = 35.12 units
    \item \textbf{Elongation Model}: Demonstrated good predictive capability (R² = 70.53\%, F = 17.15, p < 0.001) with excellent precision (RMSE = 0.42 units)
    \item \textbf{Tension Strength Model}: Showed acceptable performance (R² = 66.67\%, F = 14.33, p < 0.001) with RMSE = 5.10 units
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Critical Parameter Identification}
The statistical analysis revealed universal and quality-specific critical parameters:

\textbf{Universal Critical Parameters:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer Height}: Emerged as the most influential parameter across all quality metrics, consistently appearing in all three final models
    \item \textbf{Material Type}: Demonstrated significant impact on all quality aspects (ABS vs PLA showing distinct performance characteristics)
    \item \textbf{Temperature Control}: Both nozzle and bed temperatures proved essential for quality optimization
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Quality-Specific Parameters:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Surface Quality (Roughness)}: Print speed and thermal parameters dominate
    \item \textbf{Mechanical Strength}: Wall thickness and infill density are critical structural factors
    \item \textbf{Flexibility (Elongation)}: Infill density and print speed significantly influence material deformation characteristics
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Statistical Validation}
All models demonstrated strong statistical validity:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Highly significant F-statistics (p < 0.001) confirm genuine parameter-quality relationships
    \item Diagnostic plots validated all linear regression assumptions (linearity, independence, homoscedasticity, normality)
    \item No influential outliers detected, ensuring model robustness
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Practical Implications and Optimization Strategies}

\paragraph{Immediate Implementation Guidelines}
Based on the quantitative analysis, practitioners should prioritize:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Layer Height Optimization}: As the primary driver of quality across all metrics, precise layer height control offers maximum improvement potential
    \item \textbf{Material-Specific Parameter Profiles}: Develop distinct parameter sets for ABS and PLA materials based on their significantly different quality responses
    \item \textbf{Thermal Management Systems}: Implement precise nozzle and bed temperature control for consistent quality outcomes
    \item \textbf{Quality-Targeted Approaches}: Apply different optimization strategies depending on primary quality objectives
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Quality Assurance Framework}
The models provide a data-driven foundation for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Predictive quality control with quantified confidence levels
    \item Parameter sensitivity analysis for process optimization
    \item Quality-cost trade-off analysis using model predictions
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Contributions and Significance}

This study contributes to the 3D printing optimization field by:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Providing quantitative models with validated predictive capabilities
    \item Establishing parameter importance rankings based on statistical significance
    \item Demonstrating the effectiveness of multiple linear regression for 3D printing quality prediction
    \item Offering practical, implementable optimization strategies backed by statistical evidence
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations and Future Research Directions}

\paragraph{Current Study Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sample Size}: 50 observations limit generalizability, particularly for the tension strength model (62\% variance explained)
    \item \textbf{Scope Constraints}: Analysis limited to specific materials (ABS, PLA) and infill patterns (grid, honeycomb)
    \item \textbf{Environmental Factors}: Controlled laboratory conditions may not capture real-world variability
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Recommended Future Research}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Expanded Dataset}: Increase sample size and include additional materials, printer types, and environmental conditions
    \item \textbf{Advanced Modeling}: Explore non-linear relationships and interaction effects between parameters
    \item \textbf{Real-World Validation}: Test model predictions in industrial production environments
    \item \textbf{Multi-Objective Optimization}: Develop integrated approaches for simultaneous optimization of multiple quality metrics
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Final Remarks}

This research successfully demonstrates that multiple linear regression provides a robust, interpretable framework for understanding and optimizing 3D printing processes. The developed models offer immediate practical value for quality improvement while establishing a foundation for more sophisticated optimization approaches. The identification of layer height as a universal critical parameter and the quantification of material-specific effects provide actionable insights that can directly enhance 3D printing quality and efficiency.

The statistical rigor of this analysis, combined with its practical applicability, positions this work as a valuable contribution to evidence-based 3D printing optimization, offering both immediate implementation value and a platform for future research advancement.
